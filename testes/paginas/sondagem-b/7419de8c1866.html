We were founded nearly 40 years ago to fill a critical gap in funding for local feminist groups. Today, we are a leading funder of grassroots feminist organizations creating meaningful change that will last beyond our lifetimes.

Underfunded. Undeterred.

Around the world, rights are under attack. This isn't new, and it isn't isolated. It's a well-funded movement working to rewind decades of progress. Here's what's different today: the force, coordination, and financing behind it.

Gender-based violence is rising. Speaking out can mean arrest. Funding for gender justice is being slashed. Our partners are on the frontlines, responding every day - and we stand with them for the long haul.

What comes next requires all of us.

The People Closest to the Issues Are Closest to the Solution

Moments like this one can feel overwhelming. But backlash often follows progress, and history does not stand still. It moves when people do. Grassroots feminist organizations know what their communities need because they are part of them. They lead the solutions that work, and they need the trust and resources to do so.

HOW CHANGE HAPPENS

Grassroots feminist organizations have always worked through periods of setback, periods of stabilization, and periods of growth. Our partners aren't deploying into communities; they are already part of them. They know who is most at risk, which networks are functioning, and what kind of support will actually reach people.

We move resources directly to these organizations to advance community-driven solutions, sustained resilience, and collective power. These three pillars drive everything we fund.

Strengthened Community-Driven Solutions

Feminist organizations closest to the issues are best positioned to lead change. Flexible resources sustain and strengthen their work.

Sustained
Resilience

Grassroots feminist organizations are strong, adaptable, and resourced to continue their work over the long term, even in changing conditions.

Collective
Power

Women, girls, and gender-non-conforming people connect across borders to share knowledge and turn individual wins into lasting change.

Systems
Change

Together, these three outcomes help transform the cultural, economic, and political systems we need to advance gender justice.
