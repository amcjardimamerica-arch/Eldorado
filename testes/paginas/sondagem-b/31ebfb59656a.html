Building a Climate Just World

We fund community-led climate justice work that is rooted in lived experience, responsive to local contexts, and accountable to women, youth, and Indigenous Peoples. Our grants address climate impacts and create alternative ways of living that shift the unjust social, political, and economic systems that have generated the climate crisis.
